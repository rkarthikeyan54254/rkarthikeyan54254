# Hi 👋, I'm Rama Karthikeyan

I am an engineering leader who enjoys turning ambitious ideas into dependable products. I am especially interested in **AI systems, trustworthy knowledge products, Indian heritage, developer platforms, and consumer experiences**.

## 🚀 Currently building

### 🕉️ Evidence-backed heritage & devotional products

- **[Sandhya Katha](https://sandhyakatha.com)** · [GitHub](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/sandhyakatha) — A source-checked Hindu bedtime-story product for families. Stories from the Itihāsas, Purāṇas and related traditions are reviewed before publication, written for reading aloud, localized in English/Hindi/Tamil, and paired with age guidance, source context and a real pañcāṅga-aware nightly picker.

- **Pramāṇa** *(private core)* · **[Mahaperiyava / Deivathin Kural V1–V7 dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rkarthikeyan5/pramana-mahaperiyava-deivathin-kural-v1-v7)** — A provenance-first, source-auditable knowledge corpus for Tamil Bhakti and Sanskrit scripture. It preserves exact source identity, textual variants, verification state and evidence boundaries, then exposes only verified material to retrieval and grounded-answer products. The public Hugging Face release contains **3,368 curator-authored teaching metadata records** across Deivathin Kural Volumes 1–7 for provenance-aware retrieval, RAG, attribution and abstention evaluation, without redistributing the source text.

- **[Sri Mahaperiyava Arulurai](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/periyava)** — A consumer-facing, evidence-grounded experience for asking what Mahaperiyava taught. It sits on top of Pramāṇa's curated Deivathin Kural evidence service and deliberately preserves abstention, provenance and attribution boundaries instead of presenting generated prose as literal quotation.

- **[Nayanmar Trails](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/nayanmar-trails)** — A bilingual sacred-geography and story explorer built over versioned Pramāṇa exports: 63 Nayanmars, Tēvāram-linked sites, evidence trails, story-first discovery and an interactive Quest layer without turning tradition into unsupported historical certainty.

- **[Temple Circuit](https://templecircuit.in/)** — A mobile-first temple exploration and trip-planning experience across major Indian pilgrimage regions, with English/Tamil/Hindi guides, source-backed sthala puranam, practical visit planning, maps, saved circuits and downloadable reading guides.

- **[நல்நாள் · NalNaal](https://nalnaal.netlify.app)** · [GitHub](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/nal-naal) — A Tamil daily pañcāṅga PWA rooted in the Pambu Panchangam/Vākyam tradition, combining tithi, nakshatram, festivals, presiding deities, observance context and nearby-temple discovery in a family-friendly daily view.

- **[Abhivadhaye](https://abhivadhaye.in)** · [GitHub](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/Abhivadhaye) — A Vedic lineage salutation generator that helps families construct accurate Abhivadanam from pravara, gothra, sūtra, Veda and śākhā data, with support for English, Hindi, Tamil and Telugu.

- **[Vamsha](https://vamsha.co.in/)** — An interactive genealogy explorer for mapping family trees and professional relationships across prominent figures in Indian history and public life, with graph visualization, search and relationship-driven profile exploration.

- **[Pradosha Sivanayanmaar Archive](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/64nayanmar)** — A Tamil/English digital archive focused on Pradosha Sivanayanmaar, a prominent disciple of Kanchi Mahaperiyava, bringing together biography, photographs, schedules, videos and books.

### 📚 Publishing & digital archives

- **[Akila Karthikeyan](https://akilakarthikeyan.com)** · [GitHub](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/akilakarthikeyan) — The official digital home for Akila Karthikeyan's Tamil writing: humorous and spiritual short stories, books and audio narration, with searchable genres, editorial publishing workflows and reader engagement.

### 🎬 Consumer products & AI experiments

- **[FlickPick](https://justflickpick.netlify.app)** · [GitHub](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/flickpick) — A cinematic discovery engine designed to end the endless scroll, curating movies across Netflix, Prime Video and Zee5 with spotlight recommendations, rich film pages and direct streaming-platform handoff.

- **[Bindhify](https://bindhifyme.in)** · [GitHub](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/bindhify) — A culturally aware virtual try-on experience for bindi and tilak styles, combining face/forehead detection with adjustable overlays, live/photo workflows and privacy-conscious image handling.

- **[GenAI Digest](https://gen-ai-digest.netlify.app/)** · [GitHub](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/gen-ai-digest) — An AI-news aggregator that experiments with generation-aware curation, tailoring artificial-intelligence news and context for Gen Z, Millennials, Gen X and Boomers.

- **[Gruhapravesam Invite](https://github.com/rkarthikeyan54254/gruhapravesam-invite)** — A mobile-first digital Hindu housewarming invitation with devotional visual design, countdown, RSVP, maps and calendar integration.

## 🧭 How I like to build

I gravitate toward products where **correctness and user experience have to coexist**: evidence-backed retrieval, source provenance, multilingual UX, performance engineering, strong release gates, privacy-aware analytics, and polished consumer interfaces.
